Record 01 // Rally Reconstruction

Recovered Rules

Use all seven stroke cards exactly once. Reconstruct the rally on the terminal, then transfer each card’s landing coordinate to the court.

  1. Serve is first.
  2. Smash is last.
  3. Drop is immediately followed by Lift.
  4. Net is immediately after Clear.
  5. Clear is before Drop, but not adjacent to Drop.
  6. Drive is after Lift.
